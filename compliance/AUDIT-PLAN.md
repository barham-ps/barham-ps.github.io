# Internal audit and readiness plan
1. Approve scope, owner, policies, risk method and applicable standard editions.
2. Complete private asset/data inventory, risk treatment plan and full statement of applicability using authorized standards.
3. Review live configuration, access, synthetic authorization tests, build provenance, edge protections and recovery evidence.
4. Conduct independent internal review where practical; record findings and corrective actions privately.
5. Hold management review: risks, incidents, metrics, resources, objectives, audit results and improvements.
6. Verify closure and effectiveness of corrective actions. Do not mark missing evidence as pass.
7. Optional external certification: choose an accredited body and confirm scope, costs and audit requirements.
Cadence: weekly automated checks, monthly owner review, quarterly access and recovery review, annual internal audit and management review.
