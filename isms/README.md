# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 readiness — AL BARHAM portal
Status: draft for owner review; no certification, no formal conformity claim.
Date: 2026-11-25. This public repository contains a sanitized preparation template only. Never store personal records, secrets, internal audit findings, incident details, actual risk scores or private evidence here.

## Proposed scope (requires approval)
Operation, software development, maintenance and protection of the family portal and its membership, content and private-data services, including relevant interfaces and third-party dependencies (hosting, authentication, database, storage, code repositories and communications). The owner must identify the legal/organizational applicant, actual boundaries, locations, interested parties and requirements. Supplier certifications do not certify this portal. Determine whether climate change is relevant to the management system and whether interested parties have climate-related requirements (2024 amendment).

## Proposed policy (owner must approve)
Maintain confidentiality, integrity and availability through risk-based access, authenticated privileged operations, secure development, privacy-aware data handling, continuity planning, incident response and continual improvement. Meet applicable obligations. Provide resources, assign accountable roles, review effectiveness and document corrective actions. Do not make public certification claims until independently verified.

## Evidence mapping
Technical evidence exists for some controls: CI, CodeQL, dependency auditing, RLS, admin MFA, session safeguards, public TLS checks, file quarantine design. Review each actual result and document limitations. A failing scanner is an unresolved item, not evidence of conformity. Use the trust center and current GitHub Actions runs as evidence pointers. Full Annex A applicability review requires an authorized copy of the standard; this document is not a substitute or a complete control list.

## Certification process
1. Owner approves the scope and policy; appoints accountable personnel.
2. Obtain the current standard and amendment through legitimate access. Complete the risk assessment, treatment plan and Statement of Applicability (SoA), and collect operating evidence privately.
3. Conduct internal audit with appropriate objectivity; carry out management review and resolve nonconformities.
4. Request quotations from an independently accredited certification body with ISO/IEC 27001 scope. Verify the accreditation and certificate scope. No purchase or application is authorized by this document.
5. Complete stage 1 and stage 2 audits and any required corrective action. Only after issuance publish the authentic certificate number, issuing body, scope, validity and verification link. Maintain surveillance and renewal as required.
