## Management review
Record date, attendees, prior actions, changes to context and requirements, security performance, stakeholder feedback, risk assessment and treatment, audit findings, resources, decisions and assigned actions. Preserve approvals privately.

## Incident and recovery
Record incident detection, classification, authorized handling, containment, recovery, applicable notification assessment, evidence preservation and lessons learned. Test restoration with nonproduction data and record results. Keep incident records private.
