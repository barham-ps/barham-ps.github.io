Welcome to the Barham Family Portal

The Barham (Al Barham) family portal is a digital space for family communication, published news, heritage preservation and membership services. The portal brings together family members in Gaza and the West Bank, and those living abroad.

Our purpose

We aim to preserve family memory, make family services easier to access and strengthen communication with respect for personal privacy.

  • Explore family heritage and reviewed historical information.
  • Follow published family news and announcements.
  • Learn how to apply for membership and access approved member services.

The portal is a family initiative, not a government authority or independent proof of ancestry. The current member interface is in Arabic.
