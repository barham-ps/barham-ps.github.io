Family membership

Membership begins by creating an account and confirming your email address. The administration reviews the application before approving membership.

How to join

  1. Create an account and submit an application.
  2. Confirm your email and complete the requested information.
  3. Follow the application status while it is reviewed.

Private services require authentication and the appropriate permissions. Creating an account does not automatically grant approved membership. The application and member dashboard are currently available in Arabic.
